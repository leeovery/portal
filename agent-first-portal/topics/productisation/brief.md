# Productisation

The product and positioning layer. Free: the CLI and the desktop control plane. Pro: the mobile app, push, and the relay. Setup: ideally one line on the host that installs Portal, checks for and installs tmux if missing, starts the daemon, installs the agent hooks, and prints a pairing code, then install the app and pair; a reach-your-own-machine product, not a cloud one. Pricing posture: a small side business around 5k to 10k MRR and roughly 1000 paying users (Moshi at about 6.99/month, 59.99/year, 249 lifetime is comparison, not a decision). The agent-first repositioning and messaging: deliberately stop being agnostic and make agent-first the product thesis (adoption, and it matches how the user already uses Portal). Branding direction (indie craft executed cleanly) is leaning but not finalised. A team story is parked.
