FINDINGS:
- [in-scope] [spreading] internal/tui/preview_adapter.go:23 — The picker's Space preview reads each pane's transcript from the positional name, `state.ScrollbackFile(stateDir, SanitizePaneKey(session, window, pane))` (reached from internal/tui/pagepreview.go:276). The change-set also re-files every waiting pane's scrollback to `scrollback/pane-<token>.bin` at the first tick the pane is frozen, whether or not it has moved (internal/state/scrollback.go:123-132 renames any stored path that is not already the token path). So from that tick until the wait ends, every waiting pane previews as `(no saved content)` (internal/tui/pagepreview.go:31), not only a rearranged one. The specification accepts the preview artifact for "a waiting pane that has been rearranged". The corrigendum of 2026-09-21 added the re-file but left that accepted consequence at its original scope, so the widening was never taken as a decision. The integration suite shows the unmoved case: its subject is never moved, yet its record names `PendingScrollbackFile(token)`. There are two ways to fix it, and choosing between them is the user's decision:
  1. For a pending pane, have the preview read the token-derived name. The enumerator's `list-panes` can carry `#{@portal-pane-id}` and `#{@portal-resume-pending}`, and `state.PendingScrollbackFile` already derives the name. This needs a test that previews an unmoved waiting pane and asserts it shows the saved bytes.
  2. Widen the specification's accepted consequence to every waiting pane, with the reason.
  — FAILS: after a reboot under the shipped lazy default (the specification's own figure is about forty-one waiting panes), pressing Space in the picker on any session that holds a waiting pane shows "(no saved content)" for that pane for the whole wait. Its transcript is intact on disk under the token name, so the preview makes a false claim, on exactly the sessions this feature creates by default. Before this change-set the same pane previewed its saved transcript.
