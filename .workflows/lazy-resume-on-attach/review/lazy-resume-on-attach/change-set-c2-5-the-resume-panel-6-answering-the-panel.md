FINDINGS:
- [in-scope] [spreading] cmd/state_resume_draw.go:60 — The draw writes `hydrateAltScreenEnter` (`\e[?1049h`, cmd/state_hydrate.go:29) and paints the whole pane, assuming the pane has an alternate screen. Every answer then leaves through `hydrateResetPreamble` (`\e[?1049l`, cmd/state_resume_wait.go:378, state_resume_recover.go:46, and the backstop in state_hydrate.go:290). Nothing reads or pins tmux's `alternate-screen` option: `grep -rn alternate-screen` over the Go sources finds only test-name strings, and the specification never mentions it. The option is a documented pane option that a restored pane inherits from `set -g alternate-screen off`, and under it tmux ignores both sequences. The panel is then painted onto the primary grid, over the replayed transcript's visible screenful. Measured on a disposable tmux 3.7c `-S` server, a pane printed two real lines, entered the alternate screen, painted two card rows, left, and printed hook output. With the option on, `capture-pane -a -p` returned the two real lines under the card, and they were intact after the leave. With it off, `capture-pane -a -p` answered `no alternate screen`, and after the leave `capture-pane -p -S -` held `CARD-ROW-11` / `CARD-ROW-2hook-output`, with both real lines gone. The fix has more than one defensible form, and no unit test can observe tmux's option. One option is to pin `alternate-screen on` on the pane (`set-option -p`) from the mark step for the whole wait and unset it on every answer, tail and backstop route. Another is to read the pane's option at the mark step and resolve a pane that cannot hold an alternate screen to eager, as the existing cannot-be-marked downgrade already does. Either needs a real-tmux case that sets the option off — FAILS: lazy is the shipped default, so on any install with `alternate-screen off`, every hook-carrying pane restored after a reboot has its last screenful of transcript overwritten by the card. The card stays on screen after Enter, a confirmed discard, or a recovered waiter, and the hook's output is printed after it. Once the pending marker clears, the saver captures the card in place of that screen of transcript, so the next reboot restores the damaged history. This contradicts §5.1's "painted into the pane's alternate screen, so it never enters the scrollback" and §6.1's "nothing is wiped".
