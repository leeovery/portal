# Implementation Review: Lazy Resume On Attach

**Plan**: lazy-resume-on-attach
**Verdict**: Fail

## Summary

All 89 plan tasks across eighteen phases are implemented, none skipped or cancelled. This cycle verified the 13 tasks landed since the second review: the Phase 15 remediation of that review's planned finding (the waiting pane pinned to an alternate screen whatever the install's `alternate-screen` setting, with every route off the panel lifting the pin only once tmux confirms the leave), and the analysis-cycle work in Phases 16–18. That work covers an answered pane closing on its first exit after its binary has gone, refusals that state their reason with records that find the pane, the reshaped store records reaching their siblings, the report-row fit guard measuring the rows production emits, a discard removing only the command the confirmation showed, a stray paste never answering a waiting pane, the missed-session carry, the waiting pane carrying the token that protects its registration, and one declaration of which panes a scrollback dump skips. No task verifier raised a blocking issue. The change-set verification over every specification section and the test surface raised no finding, and it measured 48 of the 61 criteria reading could not settle, every one holding. The review fails on one item that needs planning: when the discard's input drain gives up on input that keeps arriving, the panel is painted with the terminal still echoing, so pasted lines scroll the card away and sit over it as though they ran. Two contained corrections were applied and committed — a subtest renamed to the contract it asserts, and a waiting pane carried forward from a stale index now re-filed onto its token-named transcript like a merged one. One finding predates this feature and is held out of scope.

### Needs planning
- **A3 — Drop route echoes input over the painted panel** (`cmd/state_resume_draw.go`, `cmd/state_resume_wait.go`, `cmd/tty_signals.go`, `internal/restore/lazy_resume_burst_integration_test.go`; source 17-2-1)
  - What is wrong: when the discard drain gives up (`errInputKeptArriving`), `runResumeDraw` paints the panel and execs the waiter while the tty is back in the chain's cooked mode. `ttyDrain.drain` restores the modes it found, and the chain clears only ISIG, so ECHO, ICANON and ICRNL are on. Input arriving between the paint and the waiter's raw mode is echoed onto the alternate screen, and each pasted carriage return scrolls the card. The waiter then swallows the carried arrival (`resumeWaitLoop`, `cmd/state_resume_wait.go:203-207`) and never repaints, so the panel stays scrolled and overprinted until a resize or an answer. Task 17-2 made that the route's resting state and narrowed the burst suite's assertion to the report row to accept it (`internal/restore/lazy_resume_burst_integration_test.go:241-242`, `:248`).
  - The failure: a user who presses `d` while more than a second of input streams into the pane is left with a waiting panel whose title has scrolled away and whose card is overprinted with pasted lines such as `echo 0123456789 >> burst-ran`. It reads as though those commands ran, and it stays until the pane is resized or answered.
  - How far the fix reaches: two defensible forms with different coverage, each moving a different contract. (a) Repainting when a carried arrival ends unanswered is local to `resumeWaitLoop`, but it leaves the cooked window of every draw-to-wait hand-over (resize, cancel, report, and the repaint itself) still echoing, so the case is narrowed rather than closed. (b) Echo off across the hand-over closes the window at its source, but the waiter's raw-mode restore would then put back echo-off, and every answer path (Enter and discard, through `restore()` plus the ISIG-only `EnableTTYSignals`) would hand the hook or shell a tty with echo off; echo would have to be restored on those paths, and the real-PTY mode-contract test pins exactly that ISIG-only behaviour. Only a real-PTY integration lane can observe echo.
  - Guard conditions the plan must carry: a repaint goes through `resumeRedraw` with no `DropInput` and no second flush (`TestResumeDropInput_HandOffs`, `TestFlushTTYInput_TouchesTheInputQueueInExactlyOnePlace`); echo-off is a new draw step, never a change to the drain, to hydrate's ISIG-only `DisableTTYSignals` or to the waiter's ISIG-only `EnableTTYSignals` (`TestResumeHandOffs_TerminalModes_RealPTY`, `TestTTYDrain_RealPTY`); exec stays behind `resumeHandOff`/`execHandOff` (`TestExecSeamsAreCalledOnlyByTheHandOffHelpers`); `state_resume_wait.go` names none of the draw's route (`TestRunResumeWait_Waiting`). Whichever form is chosen lands with the drop-route subtest asserting the panel title is back on screen.
