# Implementation Review: Lazy Resume On Attach

**Plan**: lazy-resume-on-attach
**Verdict**: Fail

## Summary

All 60 plan tasks across eight phases are implemented; no task verifier raised a blocking issue, and the change-set verification found the restore pipeline, the resume-mode storage and read-back, the saver's freeze, the discard route, the doctor lines and the picker indicator sound against the specification, with both test lanes and every declared linter clean on the corrected tree. The review fails on five findings whose fix shape is not settled and which go back to planning: a pending pane's scrollback re-file can move another pane's bytes over the waiting pane's token-named transcript on an interrupted tick (permanent loss); the pane appearance probe opens `/dev/tty`, which refuses a read deadline on macOS, so every panel under the shipped adaptive pair paints dark and the terminal's OSC 11 reply is echoed across the card; the picker preview shows every waiting pane as empty, wider than the specification accepted; the waiter loses a resize delivered before it arms SIGWINCH and three Alt chords swallow up to 62 following keys, with its screens harness racing a real follow window; and the capped command row can show a space the registered command does not contain. Twenty-two contained corrections — test assertions that could not fail, guards with reach gaps, two comment fixes and the `hooks.json` HTML-escaping of preserved entries — were applied, verified and committed in this session.
