# Implementation Review: Lazy Resume On Attach

**Plan**: lazy-resume-on-attach
**Verdict**: Fail

## Summary

All 76 plan tasks across fourteen phases are implemented, none skipped or cancelled. This cycle verified the 16 tasks landed since the first review — the Phase 9 remediation of that review's five planned findings, and the analysis-cycle work in Phases 10–14 (the mid-restore token match and hard-link of a moved pane's file, tokenless panes never taking a live pane's record, the one locked commit cycle, the save request surviving a tick, the commit fence outside `internal/state`, and the hydrate tails parking only the pane their mark step returned). No task verifier raised a blocking issue. The change-set verification measured the delivered tree against every section: both build lanes vet clean, `gofmt` and `golangci-lint` report nothing, every touched package passes on its own, the lazy-resume integration suites pass on real tmux (a waiting pane's resting tree measured 10–10.4 MB against the 22 MB ceiling), the macOS pane appearance probe passed 60 of 60 real-pty subtests, and 23 of 24 mutations across the load-bearing orderings were caught, the one survivor equivalent. The review fails on one finding that goes back to planning: the resume panel assumes the pane has an alternate screen, and nothing reads or pins tmux's `alternate-screen` option, so under `alternate-screen off` the card is painted over the replayed transcript's last screenful and later captured into the saved history — coupled with a refusal-table test gap that lets a mark-before-resolve reorder ship green. Four contained corrections — two false comments and two commit-cycle test defects — were applied, verified with the unit lane green, and committed in this session.

### Needs planning
- **A5 — Resume panel assumes the pane has an alternate screen** (`cmd/state_resume_draw.go`, `cmd/state_hydrate.go`, `cmd/state_hydrate_lazy_test.go`, `cmd/state_resume_wait.go`, `cmd/state_resume_recover.go`; sources 5-the-resume-panel-6-answering-the-panel-1, 14-1-1, test-surface-1)
  - What is wrong: the draw writes the alternate-screen enter (`\e[?1049h`, `cmd/state_resume_draw.go:60`) and paints the whole pane, and every answer, the recover tail and the backstop leave through the reset preamble (`\e[?1049l`). Nothing reads or pins tmux's `alternate-screen` option. Under `set -g alternate-screen off` tmux ignores both sequences and the panel is painted onto the primary grid over the replayed transcript's visible screenful — measured on a disposable tmux 3.7c socket and re-measured by the assessor, which also showed a pane-scoped `set-option -p alternate-screen on` over a global off preserves the transcript. Coupled: two refusal rows of `TestHydrateLazy_FiresTheHookWhenThePaneCannotBeMarked` ("TMUX_PANE is absent", "the executable cannot be resolved") never assert that no `@portal-resume-pending` write was issued.
  - The failure: lazy is the shipped default, so on any install with `alternate-screen off` every hook-carrying pane restored after a reboot has its last screenful of transcript overwritten by the card; the card stays after Enter, a confirmed discard or a recovered waiter, and once the marker clears the saver captures it, so the next reboot restores the damaged history. Separately, a regression writing the marker before resolving the executable ships green, and on an install where `os.Executable()` fails every lazy pane would be marked pending and exec its hook, its scrollback never written again.
  - How far the fix reaches: two defensible forms with different user-visible outcomes — (a) pin `alternate-screen on` pane-scoped from the mark step and unset it on every answer, tail and backstop route (`state_resume_wait.go:378`, `:497`; `state_resume_recover.go:46`, `:92`; the backstop at `state_hydrate.go:290`), settling the unset's order against tmux processing the leave bytes, or (b) read the pane's option at the mark step and downgrade a pane that cannot hold an alternate screen to eager through the existing refusal. Either needs a real-tmux case with the option off. The refusal table gains a per-row no-write assertion (narrowed to `@portal-resume-pending` under (a)). Guard conditions carried: every new `-t` through `tmux.PaneIDTarget`, including inside the backstop's shell argv; a new unset WARN worded apart from "unset resume pending marker failed"; the waiter and recover files keep their import bans; the real-tmux case takes `IsolateStateForTest` and `RegisterStateDirTeardownGuard` before the server starts and `//go:build integration` if it builds a binary.
