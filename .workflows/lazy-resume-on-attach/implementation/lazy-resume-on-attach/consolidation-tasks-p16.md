# Consolidation Tasks: Lazy Resume On Attach (Phase 16)

## Task 1: Every Record the Phase Reshaped Reaches Its Siblings
placement: phase 16
severity: behaviour

**Problem**: Phase 16 reshaped two families of record and stopped one sibling short in each.
- **The hooks store's refused load.** Before the phase, `Set` and `removeEntry` handled a refused load of `hooks.json` identically. The phase moved `removeEntry` (Remove and Discard) to wrap `ErrStoreRead` and write its op's WARN (`internal/hooks/store.go:225-230`), and left `Set` bare (`:150-153`): no WARN, no sentinel. `portal hook rm` against an unreadable or malformed `hooks.json` now leaves a `hooks: rm` WARN carrying the whole chain, while `portal hook set` leaves nothing in `portal.log` beyond `process: exit code=1`, since `main` prints a command error and never logs it (`main.go:53-70`). `hook set` is machine-invoked, so its stderr is read by nobody, and a registration refused this way vanishes with no trace. That breaks the audit-trail rule that every `hooks.json` mutation leaves its breadcrumb from the store method — a rule `Set` already follows for its acquire and save refusals. `errors.Is(err, ErrStoreRead)` also now holds for rm and discard but not for set, and `ErrStoreRead`'s doc lists the sites that carry it (`:326-331`), a list the phase already had to amend once.
- **The waiting pane's identity.** The phase gave every record on the wait and recovery paths the pane's durable token through `resumePaneRef.logAttrs` (`hook_key` beside `pane_key`), because the positional key restore baked names another pane, or none, after a rename, a renumber or a moved pane. The hydrate helper's `disable terminal signals failed` WARN (`cmd/state_hydrate.go:269-271`) still names the parked pane by `pane_key` alone. It is the counterpart of `enable terminal signals failed`, which now carries both, and it is the record that explains why a waiting pane's Ctrl-C ended the wait unanswered and dropped the pane to a shell with its hook never run. An operator following the pane by its token finds every other record of its wait but not this one.

**Solution**: Carry each reshaped rule to the sibling the phase left behind — derived from the store's audit-trail rule (the breadcrumb comes from the store method, for every mutation) and from the phase's own rule that every record of a waiting pane names it by its token.
- **The store.** Declare the refused-load handling once in the store and have `Set` and `removeEntry` both use it: wrap `ErrStoreRead` and write the op's WARN (`op`, `hook_key`, `via`, `error`), with `Set` passing its own method op, `set`, as its acquire-failure WARN already does. The acquire-failure WARN the two methods each write identically may share the same helper. `ErrStoreRead`'s doc stops enumerating the sites that carry it and states what it means: a read of `hooks.json` a mutation or a clean could not complete, which a failed write never carries. `internal/hooks/store_test.go`'s refusal of a malformed `hooks.json` on `Set` gains the `ErrStoreRead` assertion and the one-WARN assertion; `cmd/hooks_malformed_store_test.go` is unaffected, since `errors.Is(err, ErrMalformed)` still holds.
- **The pane.** `disable terminal signals failed` logs through `payload.paneRef().logAttrs("error", err)...`, and its test (`cmd/state_resume_signals_test.go:115`) asserts `hook_key` beside `pane_key`, as the enable side's test already does.

**Outcome**: `hook set`, `hook rm` and a panel discard refused by an unreadable `hooks.json` each leave one WARN carrying the whole chain, and each classify as `ErrStoreRead`. Every record a waiting pane's wait produces names it by its durable token.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] `Set` against a `hooks.json` it cannot read (permission denied) returns an error that `errors.Is` `ErrStoreRead`, leaves the file as it was, and leaves exactly one record at WARN or above: `hooks: set` with `op=set`, the key as `hook_key`, the caller's `via`, and the whole returned chain as `error` — naming the file and the OS's cause — with no `error_class` and no `value`.
- [ ] `Set` against a malformed `hooks.json` returns an error that `errors.Is` both `ErrMalformed` and `ErrStoreRead`, leaves the file byte-identical, and leaves exactly one WARN, `hooks: set` with `op=set`.
- [ ] `Remove` and `Discard` refused by the same unreadable or malformed `hooks.json` answer as they do today — one WARN under their own op (`rm`, `discard`) carrying `hook_key`, `via` and the whole chain, and an error that `errors.Is` `ErrStoreRead` — so all three mutations answer a refused load alike.
- [ ] A `Set`, `Remove` or `Discard` that cannot take the sidecar lock leaves the same WARN it leaves today (the existing `hookstest.AssertLockWarn` pins for `set`, `rm` and `discard` stay green).
- [ ] A `Set` whose load succeeds records exactly as it does today: `set`, `modify` and `set-noop`, and the save-failure WARN with its `value` and `error_class`.
- [ ] `portal hook set` against a malformed `hooks.json` still exits non-zero with the malformed refusal reaching stderr and the file byte-identical (`cmd/hooks_malformed_store_test.go` unchanged and green).
- [ ] A lazy pane whose signal generation cannot be turned off still parks on its chain, and its `disable terminal signals failed` WARN carries the pane's durable token as `hook_key` beside its positional `pane_key`, and the error.

**Do**:
- `internal/hooks/store.go`: declare the refused-load handling once and route `Set`'s load (`:150-153`) and `removeEntry`'s (`:225-230`) through it — it wraps `ErrStoreRead` and writes the op's WARN carrying `op`, `hook_key`, `via` and `error`, with no `error_class` and no `value`. `Set` passes `set`, its method op, as its acquire-failure WARN (`:145`) already does, never `classifySet`'s verdict, which needs the file the load failed to read.
- The acquire-failure WARN the two methods write identically (`:145`, `:220`) may route through the same helper.
- The complete set: `rg -n 's\.load\(\)|s\.acquireMutationLock\(\)' internal/hooks --glob '!*_test.go'` → 7 lines, all in `store.go`: loads at `:89` (`loadDegrading`), `:150` (`Set`), `:225` (`removeEntry`), `:384` (`deleteStale`), and acquires at `:139` (`Set`), `:216` (`removeEntry`), `:378` (`deleteStale`). `Set` and `removeEntry` — the two per-key mutations — are all of what routes through the helper. `deleteStale` keeps its acquire and load as they are, since a clean's refusal is recorded once, under the same `hooks` component, by `internal/hooksweep`'s cycle (`declinedSweep`, `internal/hooksweep/sweep.go:174-194`); `loadDegrading` is a read door, which degrades rather than refuses.
- `ErrStoreRead`'s doc (`:326-331`) states what the sentinel means — a read of `hooks.json` a mutation or a clean could not complete, which a failed write never carries — rather than listing the sites that carry it.
- `internal/hooks/store_test.go`'s "it refuses to write over a malformed hooks.json" (`:231-241`) gains the `ErrStoreRead` assertion and the one-WARN assertion. `cmd/hooks_malformed_store_test.go` is unaffected: `errors.Is(err, ErrMalformed)` still holds.
- `cmd/state_hydrate.go:270`: `disable terminal signals failed` logs through `payload.paneRef().logAttrs("error", err)...`. `TestHydrateLazy_ParksTheChainWhenSignalGenerationCannotBeCleared` (`cmd/state_resume_signals_test.go:103-119`, its record assertion at `:115-118`) asserts `hook_key` beside `pane_key`, as the enable side's tests (`TestResumeWait_SignalGenerationOnHandingThePaneOn`, `TestRunResumeRecover_SignalGeneration`) already do.
- The helper's other `pane_key`-only WARNs (`cmd/state_hydrate.go:372`, `:406`, `:416` — the pending mark's and the mid-restore clear's) are written before the pane parks and are outside this task.

## Task 2: The Report-Row Fit Guard Measures the Rows Production Emits
placement: phase 16
severity: duplication

**Problem**: `TestResumeReportRows_FitTheCard` (`cmd/state_resume_report_test.go:312-342`) is the only guard that renders the refusal rows through the card and requires each to appear whole, and its list (`:313-324`) is hand-copied literals rather than the `resumeReport*` constants that produce the rows (`cmd/state_resume_report.go:17-28`). Reword or lengthen a constant and the exact-match tests pinning that row fail and are updated in the same edit, but nothing makes the fit list move with them: it goes on rendering the old string and passing while the new row is cut on the 52-cell card — a guard checking a string nothing emits. It also covers no `resumeReportDrop` row at all. That row is 27 cells before its cause, and with the ENOTTY cause the draw tests use (`cmd/state_resume_drop_input_test.go:269`) it reads `can't clear pending input: inappropriate ioctl for device`, 57 cells, which the card cuts inside the cause — a cut the design accepts, since the act comes first, but one the guard is silent about.

**Solution**: Build the guard's rows from the production constants, so an edit to a constant changes what the guard measures — derived from the guard's own purpose, which it can only serve by measuring what production renders. The fixed rows are `resumeReportMalformed`, `resumeReportLockHeld`, `resumeReportTmuxAbsent` and `resumeReportFallback`; the prefixed rows are `resumeReportRead`, `resumeReportLock`, `resumeReportWrite`, `resumeReportLocate`, `resumeReportUnpause` and `resumeReportDrop`, each composed with a representative cause. For the drop row, where a real cause can exceed the card, the guard asserts the property the design accepts instead of wholeness: the act and its separator render whole, and any cut falls inside the cause.

**Outcome**: The fit guard fails the moment a report constant grows past the card, and it covers every row the panel can show, the drop row included.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] The guard's rows are composed from all ten `resumeReport*` constants — the four fixed rows as they stand and the six prefixes each with a representative cause — and the guard holds no row literal of its own.
- [ ] Each fixed row, and each prefixed row other than the drop row, renders whole on both the waiting panel and the discard confirmation, drawn as the guard draws them today (100×30, colourless).
- [ ] The drop row with the ENOTTY cause the draw tests use (`can't clear pending input: inappropriate ioctl for device`, 57 cells against the 52-cell card) renders on both screens with the act and its separator, `can't clear pending input: `, whole, and the cut falling inside the cause.
- [ ] A report constant lengthened past the card's 52 cells in a temporary edit — `resumeReportLockHeld`, say — fails the guard, and reverting the edit passes it.
- [ ] No production file changes, and every other test stays green.

**Do**:
- Where: `TestResumeReportRows_FitTheCard` in `cmd/state_resume_report_test.go` (`:312-342`), its literal list (`:313-324`) replaced by rows built from the constants.
- The complete set: `rg -n '^\s*resumeReport\w+\s*=' cmd/state_resume_report.go` → 10, the whole `const` block at `:17-28` — fixed rows `resumeReportMalformed`, `resumeReportLockHeld`, `resumeReportTmuxAbsent`, `resumeReportFallback`; prefixes `resumeReportRead`, `resumeReportLock`, `resumeReportWrite`, `resumeReportLocate`, `resumeReportUnpause`, `resumeReportDrop`. Every row the panel shows comes from one of them, through `resumeDiscardRefusal`, `resumeClearRefusal` or `resumeDropRefusal` (`cmd/state_resume_report.go:44-82`).
- The causes the current list pairs with each prefix stay the guard's causes — `permission denied` for read, lock and write, `no space left on device` for write, `$HOME is not defined` for locate, `can't find pane: %7` for unpause — so no row the guard measures today drops out of it. The drop row takes the ENOTTY cause the draw tests use (`cmd/state_resume_drop_input_test.go:269`).
- For the drop row the guard asserts the property the design accepts instead of wholeness: the act and its separator render whole, and any cut falls inside the cause.
