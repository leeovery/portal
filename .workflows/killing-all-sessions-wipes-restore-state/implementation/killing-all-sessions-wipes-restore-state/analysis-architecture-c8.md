SUMMARY: The save path still composes as one shared cycle. The listing failure, the own-server confirmation and the empty-capture rule are each declared once and applied identically to all three committers. The phase-9 contested-name rule fits inside the writer the cycle hands its dump: a deferred capture is written only after a landed commit, only on a name the just-committed index gives that pane alone, and never on an uncommitted end. The restore-minted tokens, the hold and the SIGTERM hardening each reuse their existing seams (RecordRestoredPaneTokens under the commit lock, hookExecArgs, paneStillPending, catchSIGTERM) rather than adding parallel copies. One candidate does not clear the floor without reversing a settled direction: commit-now, and any pane whose capture fails or is refused, commits a contested occupant's record naming its neighbour's bytes. Two things rule it out. That behaviour predates the fix on commit-now's path, and the review deliberately limited the protection to panes the dump writes, leaving every unwritten pane as it was before. The only extra harm beyond the accepted residue for a hook-less moved pane is a neighbour's history replayed in that pane, and only after a renumber just before a shutdown whose flush stands down.
