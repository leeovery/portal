  SEVERITY: medium

  FILES: cmd/completion.go:70-87, internal/tui/picker_sessions.go:13, cmd/open_search.go:131-137
  DESCRIPTION: The searched set is one rule with one home — `tui.PickerSessions(sessions, current)` — and two of its three consumers route through it (`searchCandidates` for the count, `Model.filteredSessions` for the list).
