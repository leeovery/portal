The specification (§7.6 and the 2026-09-14 / 2026-09-15 corrigenda, `.workflows/open-with-forced-filter/specification/open-with-forced-filter/specification.md:316,486,490`) makes the command-pending picker the one member of the concurrent-bootstrap set with no loading gate: it paints an actionable picker from frame one and is protected by a *hold behind* it — `createSession` stages the chosen directory while `bootstrapInFlight()`, the `BootstrapCompleteMsg` arm replays the staged mint, the notice band announces the wait, and `BootstrapFatalMsg` quits the run.
