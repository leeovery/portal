That makes the integration fixtures' pane-arming binary load-bearing evidence: a restore whose `Exe` is unpinned arms panes with the *test* binary (which stops flag parsing at the `state` positional, re-runs the suite inside the pane and exits 0), so a hook-routing assertion fails as a silently vanished session rather than an error. Phase 7 is an implementation-analysis phase, so this task's authority is its own body rather than the specification.
